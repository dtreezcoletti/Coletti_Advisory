# Coletti & Co. Service Scope v1

## Controlling business model

**Coletti & Co. — Records Reconstruction & Documentation Analysis**  
Control: `CCO-RRDA-001` · Version `1.0`

Coletti & Co. is an independent records-reconstruction and documentation-analysis firm.

> Give us the records and the question you are trying to understand; we reconstruct what the records document, what they conflict with, what is missing, and what remains unresolved.

The object of the engagement is the authorized record set. Coletti & Co. does not accept an allegation about a person as an investigative objective merely by changing terminology.

## Standard services

1. Diagnostic Records Review
2. Full Records Reconstruction
3. Transaction Reconciliation
4. Timeline & Narrative Reconstruction
5. Process & Operations Reconstruction
6. Record Conditions & Pattern Analysis
7. Professional Handoff
8. Organized Source Production
9. Recurring Records Review

## In scope

- client-controlled or client-authorized records intake;
- source inventory, indexing, classification, provenance mapping, and versioned Source Universe control;
- document and record organization, normalization, deduplication, and chronology reconstruction;
- transaction matching and numerical reconciliation;
- cross-record comparison and identification of inconsistencies;
- identification of missing supporting documentation and unresolved record questions;
- reconstruction of documented workflows, sequences, and operational history;
- identification of recurring record-supported patterns without attributing motive, intent, wrongdoing, liability, causation, or credibility;
- plain-English narrative reconstruction of what the supplied records establish, conflict with, fail to support, or leave unresolved;
- source-linked reports and timelines for client or professional review;
- Professional Handoff organized as: Professional Question → Existing Support → Conflicting Information → Missing Documentation → Unresolved Questions → Professional Determination Required;
- organized source production returned to the client at engagement close;
- recurring review of newly supplied or newly authorized records within the same records-reconstruction methodology.

## Intake and scope gate

Every prospective engagement must be screened before ordinary acceptance. The available dispositions are:

- `ACCEPT` — authorized records-reconstruction scope;
- `MODIFY_SCOPE` — the request can be converted into a records-based question;
- `PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW` — state-specific or regulated-profession review is required before acceptance;
- `DECLINE_REFER` — the requested work falls outside the standard service boundary.

Client concerns remain **Client Assertions** unless independently supported by the authorized record set.

Every accepted engagement must define a versioned **Source Universe**. Every material record finding must trace to an authorized source within that universe. Supplemental records require an authorized expansion of the Source Universe.

## Professional boundary

Coletti & Co. determines and explains the condition and relationships of records. It does not independently determine the protected professional effect of those conditions.

Coletti & Co. does not independently determine fraud, theft, negligence, breach, concealment, guilt, liability, witness credibility, motive, intent, legal causation, legal effect, accounting effect, tax effect, valuation effect, or another protected professional conclusion.

Attorneys interpret legal effect. CPAs and other qualified accounting professionals determine protected accounting effects. Licensed investigators investigate beyond the supplied record universe. Other licensed professionals determine protected effects within their disciplines.

## Collection boundary

The default source model is:

**Client supplies or expressly authorizes records → Coletti & Co. reconstructs and analyzes the authorized record set.**

Administrative retrieval may be performed only when lawful, authorized, and permitted in the applicable jurisdiction.

Standard services exclude surveillance, pretexting, witness interviews as investigators, skip tracing, covert collection, background investigations, hidden-asset searches, unauthorized account access, independent evidence development, and comparable investigative methods unless the activity is separately licensed and cleared.

## Product relationship

ColettiOS supplies generalized methodology, provenance controls, reasoning boundaries, Record States, human-review controls, and publication gates. Coletti & Co. supplies the engagement, client workflow, commercial scope, authorized Source Universe, reporting presentation, professional handoff, and client delivery.
