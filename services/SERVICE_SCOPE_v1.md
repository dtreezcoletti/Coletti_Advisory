# Coletti & Co. Service Scope v1.1

## Core service

Coletti & Co. receives fragmented records and produces a structured, source-traceable reconstruction of what the records show, where they conflict, what is missing, and what requires further professional determination.

## In scope

- document and record organization;
- source indexing and provenance mapping;
- financial-record normalization and reconciliation;
- chronology reconstruction;
- contradiction and inconsistency identification;
- missing-record / unresolved-entity identification;
- structured issue and next-hop task generation;
- source-linked reports for client or professional review;
- operational and compliance record reconstruction where the underlying standards are supplied or independently sourced.

## Out of scope by default

Coletti & Co. does not silently convert record analysis into a legal, accounting, tax, medical, investigative, engineering, or other licensed professional determination. Where an engagement reaches that boundary, the report identifies the question and the supporting source records for the appropriate licensed or authorized professional to decide.

Coletti & Co. does not determine whether client materials are admissible evidence, their evidentiary weight, legal sufficiency, or legal effect. Those determinations remain with the applicable court, regulator, licensed professional, or other authorized decision-maker.

## Product relationship

ColettiOS supplies generalized records-first methodology and technical controls. Coletti & Co. supplies the engagement, client workflow, scope, and presentation layer.
