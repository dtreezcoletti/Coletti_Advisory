# ColettiOS V1 2027 — Telephony market options and phased-budget decision

**Research date:** 2026-10-13. **State:** external-provider research and proposed rollout only. No purchases, number reservation, consent, API connection, migration, deployment, or production promotion. All listed starting rates are published pricing, not a provider quote or guaranteed account entitlement. Taxes, locality, A2P 10DLC, telephone carrier fees, per-leg billing, call forwarding, number portability, data privacy, and usage caps require checkout/acceptance validation. The business goal is a $0-first, low-maintenance phone with source-traceable C-FAB/DARI/Dispatcher follow-through. Maintain no-false-promotion rule.

## Mandatory cost caveat for automated U.S. SMS

Business-initiated SMS over U.S. 10DLC generally requires compliant A2P sender/brand/campaign registration, opt-in history, STOP/HELP and consent/suppression, plus recurring campaign and per-carrier fees. Twilio currently publishes one-time brand registration, $15 campaign vetting, and low-volume monthly campaign fees (~$1.50–$2, with eligibility/plan variations); SMS segment charges *alone* are not total SMS cost. Toll-free SMS uses separate verification/requirements, not an automatic free loophole. https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc ; https://help.twilio.com/articles/1260803965530-What-pricing-and-fees-are-associated-with-the-A2P-10DLC-service

### Three-phase cutover plan

**Phase 0 ($0 added bills):** keep public telephone as human-operated Google Voice only if current terms and account setup support its intended business use; send inquiries to portal/Square; standard business email via Resend. Run compliant forwarded-notice connector on a dedicated authorized mailbox or manual import, DARI drafts only, Dispatcher callbacks, role-restricted C-FAB. Independently test LiveKit's 1 free inbound number/50 minutes as a **noncanonical sandbox**, with synthetic/no-sensitive calls, never published as the main business number. Avoid duplicate phone numbers in public marketing.

**Phase 1 (low monthly carrier spending):** Select VoIP.ms if ready-made IVR/mobile-human calling/voicemail and less engineering matter most; choose SignalWire or Plivo if lowest number fee and custom provider-webhook programming are justified; Telnyx if extensible native DARI telephony is top priority. Activate one number, greeting, time rules, voicemail, incoming caller metadata, manually handled calls, verified outbound call status, verified failure/overflow fallback, C-FAB links and Dispatcher callback SLA. **Do not register/use automated business SMS unless approved**, instead route email and portal notifications.

**Phase 2 (revenue-backed):** Add compliant two-way SMS if users demonstrably need it; AI voice answer only after load/consent/cost acceptance, concurrency handling and budget caps; add call recording/transcription only where lawful and clinically/business appropriate; use DARI question answer with case auth; include human transfer and guaranteed voicemail fallback. A hard cap on AI usage must NOT result in a disconnected caller; carrier IVR/voicemail should stay operational independently.

## Must-verify conditions and bad paths

- Purchase/portability, number ownership/port-out guarantees, local stock, reliability and operator account verification; do not publish a disposable free demo number as your permanent business line.
- Rate card confirmed per account, number type and currency; one-time activation fees, recurring A2P and 911, carrier surcharges, API/media legs, recording and AI pricing.
- End-to-end inbound/outbound tests and voicemail retention/recording consent; calls from clients in different states require appropriate recording/AI disclosure controls.
- C-FAB source-specific events: provider-verified CDR vs Gmail notice vs human-reported callback; none of these proves SMS/email was READ; in-app authenticated view + explicit acknowledgements separately.
- DARI is identity-scoped for Owner, staff, contractors and clients; inbound **anonymous caller has no auth**. C-FAB domain role gate; no personal DOMUS export. Phone caller ID is *not* client verification.
- C-FAB currently has schema mismatch: `communication_events.transmission_state` default `RECEIVED` but CHECK excludes `RECEIVED`; fix in tested migration before registering inbound provider events, with separate role-scoped RLS.
- Fail-closed under free quota exhaustion and on provider outage; no false send/ack/delivery, no automatic voice forwarding via Google Voice, no SMS scraping.
- All enhancements remain on the pre-lock branch pending real acceptance and explicit Owner release authorization.

**Research recommendation**: compare **LiveKit free sandbox**, **VoIP.ms carrier+hosted IVR** and **SignalWire/Plivo low-cost programmable** through provider quote/testing, with Google Voice human-only as interim. Freeze a $0 Phase 0 or tiny capped phone spend; defer SMS automation and unlimited voice AI. Do not create a second C-FAB authority/agent personality.
