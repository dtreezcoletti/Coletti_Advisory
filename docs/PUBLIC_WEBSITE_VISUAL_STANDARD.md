# Coletti & Co. Public Website Visual Standard

This document is the controlling public-site design direction.

## Target feeling
The site must feel wealthy, serious, private, established, architectural, editorial, intelligent, structured, refined, modern, and non-trendy. It must not drift toward generic startup or SaaS styling.

## Composition
- 70% European private office / investment firm: ivory, bone, stone, parchment, taupe, graphite, restrained brass; elegant serif typography; whitespace; symmetry; calm photography; discreet language; understated calls to action.
- 20% architectural journal: marble, columns, stone bases, shadow lines, brass, archives, paper, shelves; editorial crops and section breaks; materiality and structure.
- 10% modern intelligence platform: crisp structured blocks, thin dividers, quiet cards, controlled iconography, subtle workflow/data rigor.

## Emotional acceptance test
The reaction should be: “This looks like a serious, expensive, highly trusted institution. They deal with complexity calmly. This feels private, intelligent, and exacting.”

## Homepage law
The landing page remains sleek and restrained. It establishes identity, scope, professional boundary, primary capabilities, process, and trusted handoff. Detail belongs in the six top-level navigation sections and nested menus.
