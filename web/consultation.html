Consultation

Start with the records. Decide from there.

A consultation is the controlled first step for determining whether Coletti & Co. is the right fit for your record problem, what needs to be reconstructed, and what the next engagement should look like.

How consultation works

01
Secure intake

Use the protected client gateway to describe the record problem and the kind of reconstruction you believe you need.

02
Pre-Consultation Assignment

Before any consultation is approved, you complete a structured assignment covering the question, known sources, gaps, conflicts, expected record volume, intended recipient, and preliminary scope.

03
Owner review

Coletti & Co. reviews the assignment and decides whether to offer a consultation, request more information, or decline the consultation.

04
Appointment & payment

If approved, the appointment and consultation fee are handled through the controlled scheduling and payment process. Full payment is required before the consultation proceeds.

05
Qualification decision

After the consultation, Coletti & Co. determines whether the matter is qualified for an engagement, needs a Record Assessment first, or is not a fit.
