Services

Operational Reconstruction

Analysis & Reconciliation

Compare records describing the same event, transaction, date, amount, process, or obligation and distinguish agreement, conflict, missing support, and unresolved issues.

Timeline & Event Reconstruction

Build source-grounded chronologies from statements, correspondence, contracts, logs, reports, messages, and other source materials.

Missing Documentation Analysis

Identify missing periods, referenced-but-unproduced records, broken chains, unexplained gaps, and incomplete supporting documentation.

Pattern & Relationship Analysis

Identify recurring, clustered, sequential, correlated, or materially consistent relationships across records without independently attributing motive, causation, wrongdoing, or liability.

Process Deviation Analysis

Compare documented procedures or expected workflows with what the records show actually occurred and identify deviations requiring further review.

Business Records Intelligence

Help small and medium-sized businesses understand fragmented financial and operational records, vendor histories, payment activity, process breakdowns, documentation gaps, and handoff failures.

Complex Matter Review

Review large or disorganized bodies of records and surface the material issues that require human or professional attention.

Professional Support

Carry the reconstruction burden so attorneys, accountants, fiduciaries, insurance professionals, and other qualified advisers can focus on interpretation and professional judgment.

Recurring Records Review
