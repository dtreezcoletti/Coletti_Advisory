Services

Business Record Intelligence, bounded by the record.

We work from lawfully supplied or expressly authorized records. We reconstruct what those records document, where they conflict, what is missing, and what remains unresolved.

Record Diagnostic

A bounded first-stage review of the record set’s condition, organization, scope, completeness, usability, documentation gaps, conflicts, duplicates, and reconciliation opportunities.

Business Reconstruction

Source inventory, indexing, classification, normalization, chronology, cross-record comparison, reconciliation, missing-document identification, pattern analysis, and reconstruction of documented workflows and sequences.

Transaction Reconstruction

Compare dates, amounts, account identifiers, invoices, statements, payroll, receipts, payments, transfers, ledgers, and other supplied records to identify matches, differences, unsupported entries, duplicates, timing differences, and unresolved discrepancies.

Timeline & Reconstruction Narrative

Reconstruct the documented chronology and explain in plain English what the records affirmatively document, what can be reconciled, what conflicts, what remains unsupported, and what cannot be resolved.

Operations Reconstruction

Reconstruct a process, workflow, sequence, or operational history reflected in supplied records and compare the documented record trail with supplied policies, procedures, schedules, agreements, or stated workflows.

Findings & Record Conditions

Identify documented facts, reconciliation results, inconsistencies, missing documentation, process deviations, recurring patterns, unresolved questions, client assertions, third-party conclusions, and matters requiring professional referral.

Professional Handoff

Organize the professional question, existing support, conflicting information, missing documentation, unresolved questions, and source citations so the appropriate qualified professional can determine the protected professional effect.

Indexed Chronological Record Package

Return the engagement source universe in an organized, labeled, indexed, and categorized production together with the applicable source manifest and published reports.

Ongoing Record Intelligence

Periodically process newly supplied or newly authorized records using the same controlled reconstruction methodology without becoming surveillance, monitoring of an individual, or an independent investigation.

What We Do Not Do

Coletti & Co. does not independently investigate individuals, determine fraud or wrongdoing, assess witness credibility, determine motive or intent, render legal or accounting opinions, conduct surveillance, perform hidden-asset searches, use covert techniques, or independently develop evidence. Requests that cross those boundaries are modified, reviewed, declined, or referred as appropriate.
