For Professionals

We reconstruct the record. You determine what it means.

Our professional-support model is designed to remove the time-intensive reconstruction burden while preserving professional independence.

Professional Handoff Packet

A structured handoff can include the reconstructed record, source index, chronology, reconciliation results, inconsistencies, missing documentation, unresolved questions, identified patterns, process deviations, supporting schedules, and referral-required items. The receiving professional retains responsibility for protected professional interpretation.

Attorneys

Source-grounded chronology, record organization, conflict analysis, missing-document identification, and review-ready supporting materials. We do not give legal opinions or determine liability, admissibility, or legal strategy.

Accountants / CPAs

Transaction and statement reconstruction, cash-flow chronology, document organization, and reconciliation support. We do not provide accounting, tax, audit, valuation, or attestation opinions.

Fiduciaries & Advisers

Structured records and operational histories that reduce the time required to understand a complex source universe before professional judgment is applied.

Insurance Professionals

Organized records, chronology, supporting documentation, and inconsistency analysis. We do not determine coverage, claims liability, actuarial effect, or insurance obligations.

Other Licensed Professionals

The same boundary applies across disciplines: Coletti & Co. may determine and describe the condition of the records, but it does not independently determine the protected professional effect of that condition.
