About

A private institution for difficult records.

Coletti & Co. exists to bring disciplined reconstruction to matters where records are fragmented, contradictory, incomplete, or too complex to understand efficiently.

The Firm

Independent records reconstruction and operational intelligence, delivered with discretion, precision, source discipline, and a clear professional boundary.

ColettiOS

Our internal records-intelligence operating system supports provenance, record-state discipline, contradiction handling, reconciliation, review controls, and professional-ready output. The system is the operational rigor beneath the firm; the client experience remains calm and human.

Our Principles

Source first. No silent increase in certainty. Contradictions remain visible. Reconciliation is traceable. Human review stays distinct from the underlying records. Professional effect belongs to the appropriate professional.

First Truth Standard

We start with the condition of the records, not with a desired claim, narrative, or outcome.

Why Coletti & Co.

Complexity often consumes professional time before the professional question can even be addressed. Our purpose is to remove that reconstruction burden and deliver a disciplined record that can be understood, reviewed, and handed off intelligently.
