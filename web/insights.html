Insights

Records intelligence, explained clearly.

Brief, practical explanations of reconstruction, record condition, professional handoff, and the difference between what records show and what a professional concludes from them.

Records

What is records reconstruction?

It is the disciplined process of turning a fragmented source universe into a coherent, traceable record without silently filling gaps or promoting assumptions into facts.

Operations

What happened versus what was supposed to happen.

Operational reconstruction compares the record of an actual workflow or event sequence with the documented or expected process.

Financial

Financial reconstruction is not accounting advice.

We can reconstruct statements, transactions, cash flow, and supporting records. A qualified accounting, tax, valuation, or other professional determines the protected professional effect.

Professionals

The handoff should begin with a usable record.

A professional should be able to see chronology, sources, conflicts, gaps, reconciliations, and unresolved questions without rebuilding the source universe from scratch.

Glossary

Record States matter.

Documented Fact, Reconciliation Result, Inconsistency, Missing Documentation, Process Deviation, Unresolved Question, Client Assertion, Third-Party Conclusion, Referral Required, and Identified Pattern are deliberately distinct states.
