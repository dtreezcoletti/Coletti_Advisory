Professional Boundary Standard

The record and the professional opinion are not the same thing.

Coletti & Co. is designed to preserve that distinction.

Our Role

Coletti & Co. provides records reconstruction, operational reconstruction, records intelligence, and professional-support services. We are not a law firm, accounting firm, tax advisory firm, insurance professional, investment adviser, medical provider, or other licensed protected professional.

Our work may identify the condition, relationships, inconsistencies, gaps, chronology, process deviations, reconciliation results, unresolved questions, or patterns within a body of records. We do not independently determine the legal, accounting, tax, insurance, medical, regulatory, investment, valuation, or other protected professional effect of those findings.

Controlling Principle

ColettiOS can determine the condition of the records; it cannot independently determine the protected professional effect of that condition.

When protected professional interpretation is required, the work is prepared for referral or handoff to the appropriate qualified professional.
