Professional Independence

A clear boundary.

We reconstruct the records. We do not investigate individuals or independently determine fraud, wrongdoing, liability, credibility, motive, intent, or other protected professional conclusions. ColettiOS can determine the condition of the records; it cannot independently determine the protected professional effect of that condition.
