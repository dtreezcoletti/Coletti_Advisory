Our Approach

Reconstruction before interpretation.

We establish the condition of the records first: what exists, what connects, what conflicts, what is missing, what can be reconciled, and what remains unresolved.

What We Do

Coletti & Co. reconstructs complex record sets and operational histories into a controlled, source-grounded structure. Our role is to make the record usable, not to replace the professional whose discipline determines the protected meaning of that record.

How Reconstruction Works

  • Define scope and source universe.
  • Organize, normalize, and preserve provenance.
  • Reconstruct chronology and record relationships.
  • Reconcile what can be reconciled.
  • Surface conflicts, gaps, deviations, and unresolved questions.
  • Deliver a clear reconstruction and professional handoff where appropriate.

Record States & Findings

Our work distinguishes documented facts from reconciliation results, inconsistencies, missing documentation, process deviations, unresolved questions, client assertions, third-party conclusions, referral-required items, and identified patterns.

Security & Confidentiality

Sensitive matters require controlled access, traceability, source lineage, and deliberate publishing. The public website communicates the firm; client records belong inside controlled client and staff workflows.

First Truth Standard

We do not begin with the story someone wants the records to tell. We begin with what the records actually support, what they do not support, and what still requires qualified professional interpretation.
